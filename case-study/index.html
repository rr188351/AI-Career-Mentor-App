PROJECT OVERVIEW

Turning an open-ended decision into a guided journey.

THE PROBLEM

Career seekers face too many options, generic advice and no clear way to understand what to do next.

The product concept combines a concise profile, transparent career matches and a milestone-based roadmap without presenting recommendations as guarantees.

Primary personaAnanya Sharma, 21

Final-year Computer Science student

Target audienceStudents to career switchers

Also recent graduates and early-career professionals

Core experienceDiscover → Understand → Act

Assessment, recommendations, comparison and roadmap

PRIMARY PERSONA

Ananya Sharma

21 · Final-year Computer Science student

“I know I want a successful career, but I need clarity on which path is right for me.”
Fictional, persona-based profile

Goals

  • Find paths that match her interests and skills
  • Identify gaps and prepare for industry
  • Build an actionable career roadmap

Frustrations

  • Conflicting online advice
  • Generic recommendations
  • Unclear learning paths and progress

Behaviours

  • Researches through Google, YouTube and LinkedIn
  • Compares options before deciding
  • Prefers visual, concise guidance

Design implication

Make every match understandable and always connect insight to a practical next action.

UI CASE STUDY

A calm, cinematic system built around trust and progress.

Near-black navy surfaces establish focus. Cyan communicates action and progress, while violet is reserved for AI guidance and reflective moments.

01

Hierarchy before decoration

The mentor card and first career match receive the strongest depth; secondary cards remain restrained.

02

Glass with a purpose

Translucency separates layers and navigation without coating every element in blur.

03

Motion as feedback

Entrances clarify sequence; selection pops, progress fills and save feedback confirm state.

04

Accessible restraint

Readable contrast, 44-pixel targets, focus-visible states and reduced-motion support are built in.
