
# Project 01 — AI Career Mentor

## 1. Project Goal

- Develop an AI-powered career guidance mobile application that helps students and professionals identify suitable career paths based on their interests, skills, strengths, and goals.
- Simplify career exploration through personalized assessments, AI-powered recommendations, and structured career roadmaps.
- Help users make informed career decisions instead of relying on random online advice.
- Create a seamless user experience that transforms career confusion into clear, actionable next steps.

## 2. Target Audience

- College Students: Students exploring career options, internships, and future specializations.
- Recent Graduates: Graduates seeking entry-level jobs and guidance on industry requirements.
- Career Switchers: Professionals interested in transitioning to a different field.
- Early-Career Professionals: Users looking to identify skill gaps and plan career growth.
- Undecided Learners: People who need structured guidance to understand their interests, abilities, and potential career paths.

## 3. General Tasks

- Create an account and complete an onboarding questionnaire.
- Identify interests, skills, strengths, preferences, and career goals.
- Complete an AI-powered career assessment.
- Receive personalized career recommendations with clear explanations.
- Explore career details, including required skills, qualifications, and potential career paths.
- Interact with an AI Career Mentor for career-related questions.
- View a personalized dashboard and track assessment results and progress.
- Follow a structured learning and career roadmap.
- Save preferred careers, review recommendations, and update personal goals.

## 4. Cross Channels

- Mobile Application: Primary experience for onboarding, assessments, recommendations, AI chat, and progress tracking.
- Tablet Experience: Adaptable layouts for career exploration, dashboards, and detailed roadmap views.
- Responsive Web Experience: Accessible career guidance and assessment results across desktop and laptop screens.
- Consistent Experience: Maintain visual consistency, user preferences, progress, and navigation patterns across supported devices.

## 5. Technology

- Design & Prototyping: Figma and Figma Make AI.
- UX Research & Planning: User personas, empathy maps, journey maps, competitor analysis, and user flows.
- UI Design: Design system, reusable components, typography, color palette, icons, and responsive layouts.
- AI Interaction Concept: Conversational AI mentor, personalized recommendations, and assessment-based career insights.
- Prototyping & Testing: Interactive prototypes, usability testing, accessibility checks, and iterative design improvements.

Note: These are proposed design technologies and conceptual product capabilities, not claims that a production AI backend has already been implemented.

## 6. Critical Success Factors

- Personalization: Recommendations should reflect each user's interests, skills, goals, and assessment responses.
- Clarity: Career insights and next steps should be easy to understand, even for first-time users.
- Trust & Transparency: Explain why a career is recommended, avoid unsupported claims, and protect users' personal information.
- Ease of Use: Keep onboarding and assessments intuitive, with minimal unnecessary friction.
- Actionable Guidance: Convert recommendations into achievable learning milestones and career roadmaps.
- Visual Consistency: Maintain a polished, cohesive interface across all screens and device sizes.
- Accessibility: Ensure readable typography, sufficient contrast, clear navigation, and accessible interaction states.
- Engagement: Help users track progress, revisit goals, and continue their career exploration.
- Usability Validation: Test core user journeys and improve the design based on actual user feedback.

## 4. Sample UX insights

- Users may need help narrowing down career options instead of receiving an overwhelming list.
- Career recommendations should explain why each career matches the user's profile.
- A structured roadmap with small milestones can make career planning feel more manageable.
- Interactive career mini-tasks could help users explore a profession before committing to it.

# User Journey Map — AI Career Mentor

Persona: Ananya Sharma · 21 years · Final-year Computer Science Student

### 1. Onboarding & Discovery

Goal: Understand the app and start confidently.

Actions

- Signs up and creates her profile.
- Selects interests, skills and career goals.
- Completes the initial career assessment.

Touchpoints: Welcome screen, onboarding questionnaire, assessment.

Thoughts: “Will this app actually help me find the right career?”

Emotions: Curious but uncertain.

Pain points: Lengthy forms and unclear questions.

Opportunity: Short onboarding, progress indicator and clear explanation of how data will be used.

### 2. AI Mentor & Career Exploration

Goal: Discover suitable career paths.

Actions

- Reviews personalized career recommendations.
- Asks the AI mentor questions.
- Explores career requirements and compares different roles.

Touchpoints: AI chat, career cards, recommendation details, career comparison.

Thoughts: “Why is this career a good match for me?”

Emotions: Interested and hopeful.

Pain points: Generic advice and unexplained recommendations.

Opportunity: Explain each match using her skills, interests and assessment responses.

### 3. Career Roadmap & Action

Goal: Turn recommendations into practical steps.

Actions

- Chooses a preferred career direction.
- Reviews required skills and learning milestones.
- Starts completing roadmap activities.

Touchpoints: Career roadmap, skill-gap analysis, task checklist, learning resources.

Thoughts: “I finally know what I should do next.”

Emotions: Motivated but slightly overwhelmed.

Pain points: Too many tasks and unrealistic expectations.

Opportunity: Break goals into small, manageable milestones with alternative learning paths.

### 4. Progress Review & Growth

Goal: Understand progress and improve skills.

Actions

- Reviews completed milestones.
- Checks skill development and remaining gaps.
- Updates goals based on progress.

Touchpoints: Progress dashboard, achievements, skill tracker, roadmap review.

Thoughts: “Am I getting closer to becoming job-ready?”

Emotions: Proud when progressing; frustrated when stuck.

Pain points: Progress feels invisible or difficult to measure.

Opportunity: Show meaningful progress indicators, celebrate milestones and suggest the next useful action.

### 5. Community, Feedback & Support

Goal: Get help and learn from others.

Actions

- Shares questions or challenges with peers.
- Gives feedback on recommendations and app usability.
- Reports issues or requests help when needed.

Touchpoints: Community discussions, feedback forms, help centre, support chat.

Thoughts: “I want reliable advice when I get stuck.”

Emotions: Connected when supported; frustrated when answers are unclear.

Pain points: Unanswered questions and unreliable advice.

Opportunity: Helpful moderation, clear support pathways and easy-to-access feedback.

### 6. Long-Term Career Relationship

Goal: Keep growing as career goals evolve.

Actions

- Revisits career goals and explores new opportunities.
- Updates skills and preferences.
- Uses the AI mentor for interview preparation and future transitions.

Touchpoints: Personalized dashboard, goal reminders, updated recommendations, career preparation tools.

Thoughts: “I can keep using this as my career evolves.”

Emotions: Confident, supported and independent.

Pain points: Repetitive recommendations and irrelevant notifications.

Opportunity: Adapt guidance to changing goals, offer useful reminders and allow users to control their notification preferences.
